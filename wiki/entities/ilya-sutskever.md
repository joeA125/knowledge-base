# Ilya Sutskever

Researcher at [[google-brain]]. Co-author of [[rnn-regularisation|Recurrent Neural Network Regularization]] (2014) and co-developer of sequence-to-sequence learning with neural networks (Sutskever et al., 2014). A key figure in the development of modern deep learning.
