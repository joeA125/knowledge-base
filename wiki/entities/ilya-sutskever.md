# Ilya Sutskever

Researcher at [[google-brain]] and later [[openai]]. Co-author of [[rnn-regularisation|Recurrent Neural Network Regularization]] (2014), co-developer of sequence-to-sequence learning (Sutskever et al., 2014), and co-author of the [[variational-lossy-autoencoders|Variational Lossy Autoencoder]] (2017). A key figure in the development of modern deep learning.
