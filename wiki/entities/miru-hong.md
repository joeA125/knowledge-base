# Miru Hong

Researcher in the Department of Artificial Intelligence, University of Seoul. Lead author of [[scoutgpt-counterfactual-player-valuation|Modeling Matches as Language]] (2026), which introduced [[scoutgpt]] for [[counterfactual-simulation]] of football player transfers.

Also lead author of EventGPT (Hong et al., 2025), the group's earlier generative framework for capturing player impact from team action sequences. ScoutGPT extends it from short generated fragments — which forced the remaining value to be approximated — to full episode generation under hypothetical lineups.
