# Miru Hong

Researcher in the Department of Artificial Intelligence, University of Seoul.

**Joint first author** (with [[minho-lee]], equal contribution) of [[eventgpt-player-impact-team-action-sequences|EventGPT]] (2025), and lead author of [[scoutgpt-counterfactual-player-valuation|ScoutGPT]] (2026). Together these constitute the vault's coverage of generative [[counterfactual-simulation]] for football transfer evaluation.

The progression between the two papers is instructive: EventGPT established player-conditioning and the [[on-ball-value|residual OBV]] target while re-scoring fixed sequences; ScoutGPT added lineup conditioning, [[constrained-decoding]], and full sequence *re-generation* — a stronger counterfactual.
