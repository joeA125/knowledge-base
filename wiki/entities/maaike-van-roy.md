# Maaike Van Roy

Researcher in the Department of Computer Science at KU Leuven. Lead author of [[on-ball-actions-football-xt-vs-vaep|Valuing On-the-Ball Actions in Soccer: A Critical Comparison of xT and VAEP]] (AAAI 2020), the first direct comparison of [[expected-threat|xT]] and [[vaep]] as [[action-valuation]] frameworks.

Supported at the time by the Research Foundation–Flanders (EOS No. 30992574). Part of the KU Leuven soccer analytics group alongside [[tom-decroos]], [[pieter-robberechts]], and [[jesse-davis]].
