# Pascal Bauer

Holder of the Chair for Sports Analytics at Saarland University and affiliated with the **Deutscher Fussball-Bund (DFB)**, the German Football Association. Co-author of [[scoutgpt-counterfactual-player-valuation|Modeling Matches as Language]] (2026).

The DFB affiliation is notable: this is one of the few sources in the vault with a direct line into a national federation's practice, rather than purely academic or club-vendor work. It suggests the [[counterfactual-simulation]] framing is being considered for real recruitment decisions and not only as a modelling exercise.

Also co-author of "A Goal Scoring Probability Model for Shots Based on Synchronized Positional and Event Data" (Anzer & Bauer, 2021) — cited across this vault as a modern [[expected-goals]] model combining tracking and event data, and used as a reference point by [[lpv|Hirnschall & Bajons]] when justifying their simpler xG proxy.
