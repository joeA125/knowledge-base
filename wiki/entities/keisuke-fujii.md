# Keisuke Fujii

Researcher at the Graduate School of Informatics, Nagoya University, with affiliations at the RIKEN Center for Advanced Intelligence Project (Fukuoka) and JST PRESTO. Senior author of [[transformer-point-process-football-event-modelling|Transformer-Based Neural Marked Spatio Temporal Point Process Model for Football Match Events Analysis]] (2023), which introduced [[nmstpp]] and [[hpus]].

Leads a broader programme in sports analytics and trajectory modelling. Related work cited in the NMSTPP paper includes evaluating scoring-opportunity creation for teammates via trajectory prediction (Teranishi et al., 2022), defensive evaluation via ball-recovery prediction models (Toda et al., 2022), and generalised defensive valuation applied to UEFA EURO tournaments (Umemoto et al., 2022) — a line of work explicitly aimed at the defensive blind spot shared by [[vaep]], [[expected-threat|xT]], and NMSTPP itself.
