# Jianhui Chen

Researcher at the University of British Columbia. Lead author of [[sports-camera-calibration-synthetic-data|Sports Camera Calibration via Synthetic Data]] (2019), which introduced the 3-free-parameter camera pose engine and [[siamese-network]]-based retrieval for sports [[camera-calibration]]. The Chen & Little method became a foundational baseline, referenced across multiple vault papers ([[tvcalib-camera-calibration-football|TVCalib]], [[soccernet-v2-action-spotting|CCBV-SN]], [[amateur-football-analytics-computer-vision|Mavrogiannis thesis]]).
