# Sang-Ki Ko

Faculty member in the Department of Artificial Intelligence, University of Seoul, and senior author of [[scoutgpt-counterfactual-player-valuation|Modeling Matches as Language]] (2026).

Leads a group working on generative modelling of football event streams, spanning EventGPT (2025), the VERSA verified event data format (Jo et al., 2026), and [[scoutgpt]]. The trajectory runs from data quality, through generative modelling, to [[counterfactual-simulation]] for recruitment — a programme rather than a single paper.
