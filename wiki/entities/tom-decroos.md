# Tom Decroos

Researcher at KU Leuven, Belgium. Lead author of [[evaluating-football-player-actions|Actions Speak Louder than Goals]] (KDD 2019), which introduced [[spadl]] and [[vaep]] for valuing individual player actions in soccer.

Also co-author of [[on-ball-actions-football-xt-vs-vaep|Valuing On-the-Ball Actions in Soccer]] (AAAI 2020) — a critical comparison of VAEP against [[expected-threat|xT]] that reports findings unfavourable to his own model, notably VAEP's much weaker [[split-half-reliability|split-half reliability]].

Earlier work includes soccer highlight prediction from spatio-temporal event streams (AAAI 2017), the STARSS spatio-temporal action rating system (2017), and player vectors for characterising playing style (2019) — the last of which supplies the finding, cited in the xT/VAEP comparison, that players are highly consistent in which action types they perform at which locations.

Supported by the Research Foundation–Flanders (FWO).
