# Tom Decroos

Researcher at KU Leuven, Belgium. Lead author of [[evaluating-football-player-actions|Actions Speak Louder than Goals]] (KDD 2019), which introduced [[spadl]] and [[vaep]] for valuing individual player actions in soccer. Supported by the Research Foundation-Flanders (FWO). Also authored earlier work on soccer highlight prediction (AAAI 2017) and automatic tactic discovery (KDD 2018).
