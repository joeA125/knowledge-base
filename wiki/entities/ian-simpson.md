# Ian Simpson

Lead author of [[seq2event]] (Simpson, Beal, Locke & Norman, KDD 2022), which framed football event forecasting as "learning the language of soccer" and introduced the poss-util possession metric. Seq2Event is the direct predecessor to [[nmstpp]] and its principal baseline.

Acknowledged by [[calvin-yeung]] and colleagues for "fruitful discussions about football event data modeling."
