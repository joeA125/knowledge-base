# Calvin C. K. Yeung

Researcher at the Graduate School of Informatics, Nagoya University, Japan. Lead author of [[transformer-point-process-football-event-modelling|Transformer-Based Neural Marked Spatio Temporal Point Process Model for Football Match Events Analysis]] (2023), which introduced [[nmstpp]] and the [[hpus]] metric.

Supported by JST SPRING (JPMJSP2125) and the Interdisciplinary Frontier Next-Generation Researcher Program of the Tokai Higher Education and Research System. Maintains the model's public implementation.
