# Marc Van Droogenbroeck

Professor at the University of Liège, Belgium. A leading figure in sports computer vision and [[camera-calibration]]. Co-author of [[soccernet-v2-action-spotting|Camera Calibration and Player Localization in SoccerNet-v2]] (Cioppa et al., 2021) and [[camera-calibration-benchmarking|ProCC: A Universal Protocol to Benchmark Camera Calibration for Sports]] (Magera et al., 2025). Also involved in the broader SoccerNet ecosystem (tracking, game state reconstruction, depth estimation).
