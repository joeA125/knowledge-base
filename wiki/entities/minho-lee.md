# Minho Lee

Researcher at the Institute for Sports and Preventive Medicine, Saarland University.

**Joint first author** (with [[miru-hong]], equal contribution) of [[eventgpt-player-impact-team-action-sequences|EventGPT]] (2025), which introduced player-conditioned event generation and the [[on-ball-value|residual OBV]] target. Also co-author of [[scoutgpt-counterfactual-player-valuation|ScoutGPT]] (2026) and the VERSA verified event data format.

His Saarland affiliation places him alongside [[pascal-bauer]] on the sports-science side of a collaboration otherwise led from the University of Seoul's AI department — a pairing that recurs across the group's output.

> **Correction:** an earlier version of this page, written from ScoutGPT's author list alone, described him as a minor co-author. The primary EventGPT source shows he is joint first author of the foundational paper in this line.
