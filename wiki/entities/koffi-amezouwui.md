# Koffi Amezouwui

Researcher at Univ. Rennes, Ensai, CNRS, CREST-UMR 9194. Corresponding author of [[football-event-sequences-point-process-mixture|Model-Based Clustering of Football Event Sequences]] (2025), which introduced a [[mixture-model]] of marked spatio-temporal [[point-process]]es for clustering whole football possessions.

Work funded by the French Agence Nationale de la Recherche (ANR-19-STPH-004) within the PIA EUR DIGISPORT project.
