# Luís Meireles

Researcher affiliated with the [[universidade-do-porto|Faculdade de Engenharia, Universidade do Porto]] and with [[fc-porto|FC Porto — Futebol SAD]], the club's professional football arm.

Second author of [[football-performance-time-series|"Valuing Players Over Time"]], and a consistent collaborator with [[tiago-mendes-neves|Mendes-Neves]] and [[joao-mendes-moreira|Mendes-Moreira]] — the same trio behind the [[large-event-model|Large Event Model]] line of work.

## The Club Affiliation

The FC Porto affiliation is notable and unusual. Most football-analytics research in this vault comes from purely academic groups working on purchased or public event data ([[jesse-davis|KU Leuven]], [[keisuke-fujii|Nagoya]], [[sang-ki-ko|Kangwon/K League]]). A co-author employed by the club whose league is being analysed suggests direct access to practitioner requirements.

This plausibly explains the strongly applied character of the time-series paper. Its use cases — consistency as a selection criterion, locating a player on a [[player-development-curve|development curve]], identifying underpriced late bloomers — read as recruitment-department questions rather than methodological ones. The paper's acknowledgements record separate funding from FC Porto — Futebol SAD for the second author, alongside a disclaimer that the views are the authors' own.
