# FC Porto — Futebol SAD

The corporate arm of Futebol Clube do Porto, one of Portugal's largest football clubs. *Futebol SAD* (*Sociedade Anónima Desportiva*) is the Portuguese legal form for a club's professional football operation held as a public limited sports company.

## Relevance to This Vault

FC Porto is the only professional club appearing as a **co-author affiliation** in the vault's football-analytics sources: [[luis-meireles]] is affiliated to it on [[football-performance-time-series|"Valuing Players Over Time"]], and the acknowledgements record the club funding his contribution, with the usual disclaimer that the views expressed are the authors' own.

This makes the paper an unusual artefact — football-analytics research produced partly *inside* a club rather than about clubs from outside.

## Why the Affiliation Shows in the Work

The paper's four use cases map onto recruitment-department problems rather than research questions:

| Use case | Club problem it addresses |
|---|---|
| Long-term rating trajectories | Is this target improving or declining? |
| Per-action-type series | Has this player's role or style changed? |
| [[performance-volatility\|Volatility]] metrics | Can we rely on him week to week? |
| [[player-development-curve\|Development curve]] and late bloomers | Are we paying peak-age prices for post-peak players? |

The late-bloomer application is explicitly a **market-inefficiency** argument: since valuation falls sharply after the nominal peak age of 25–27, players who peak later are systematically underpriced. That is a scouting strategy stated in a research paper.

The vault's other football sources — [[jesse-davis|KU Leuven]], [[keisuke-fujii|Nagoya]], [[karun-singh|independent analysts]] — are academic or public-facing, and their evaluation criteria run to held-out likelihood, [[split-half-reliability|reliability]], and interpretability. This one is oriented toward decisions.
