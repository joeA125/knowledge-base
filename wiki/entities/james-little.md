# James J. Little

Professor at the University of British Columbia. Co-author of [[sports-camera-calibration-synthetic-data|Sports Camera Calibration via Synthetic Data]] (Chen & Little, 2019). The Chen & Little method is a foundational baseline for sports [[camera-calibration]], referenced across [[tvcalib-camera-calibration-football|TVCalib]], [[soccernet-v2-action-spotting|CCBV-SN]], and the [[amateur-football-analytics-computer-vision|Mavrogiannis thesis]].
