# Tony Sit

Researcher in the Department of Statistics at the Chinese University of Hong Kong. Co-author of [[transformer-point-process-football-event-modelling|Transformer-Based Neural Marked Spatio Temporal Point Process Model for Football Match Events Analysis]] (2023), which introduced [[nmstpp]] and [[hpus]].

The statistical grounding of the work — the [[point-process]] formulation and the chain-rule factorisation of the joint conditional density — reflects the statistics-side contribution to an otherwise informatics-led collaboration.
