# Kazuya Takeda

Researcher at the Graduate School of Informatics, [[nagoya-university]]. Co-author of [[creating-scoring-opportunities-trajectory-prediction|the C-OBSO paper]] and of the earlier Teranishi, Fujii & Takeda (2020) trajectory-prediction work at IEEE GCCE — so he appears on both ends of the group's trajectory line.

Also co-author with [[keisuke-fujii]] on the cited-but-not-held *Policy learning with partial observation and mechanical constraints for multi-person modeling* (arXiv:2007.03155), which sits in the same [[imitation-learning|imitation-learning]] territory as the [[trajectory-prediction]] models used here.

The recurrence across the group's trajectory papers, but not its event-classification ones ([[vdep]], [[nmstpp]]), suggests the movement-modelling side is his contribution — though this is inference from co-authorship patterns rather than anything stated.
