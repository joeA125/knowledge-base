# Karun Singh

Creator of [[expected-threat]] (xT), introduced in a 2019 blog post rather than a peer-reviewed venue. Despite that informal publication route, xT was rapidly adopted across the soccer analytics community and is treated by [[on-ball-actions-football-xt-vs-vaep|Van Roy et al. (2020)]] as the canonical exemplar of possession-based [[action-valuation]].

The model's traction is a notable case of practitioner-originated work becoming a research baseline — its appeal owing largely to conceptual simplicity and [[interpretability]] rather than to sophistication.
