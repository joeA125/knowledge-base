# Universidade do Porto

Portuguese public research university. The football-analytics work in this vault comes from its **Faculdade de Engenharia** (FEUP, the engineering faculty), typically in joint affiliation with [[inesc-tec|LIAAD — INESC TEC]].

## Football Analytics at Porto

The group of [[tiago-mendes-neves]], [[luis-meireles]] and [[joao-mendes-moreira]] is the vault's only research group with a **direct professional club affiliation** ([[fc-porto|FC Porto — Futebol SAD]]) alongside its academic base.

Its output spans both the applied and foundational ends of football event modelling:

| Work | Character |
|---|---|
| [[football-performance-time-series\|Valuing Players Over Time]] | Applied — [[player-rating-time-series\|time-series]] treatment of player ratings for recruitment |
| [[large-event-model\|Large Event Models]] | Foundational — generative transformers over tokenised match events |
