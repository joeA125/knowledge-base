# Pieter Robberechts

Researcher in the Department of Computer Science at KU Leuven. Co-author of [[on-ball-actions-football-xt-vs-vaep|Valuing On-the-Ball Actions in Soccer: A Critical Comparison of xT and VAEP]] (AAAI 2020).

Also co-authored work on in-game win probability modelling for soccer (Robberechts, Van Haaren & Davis, 2019), which informs the game-context features used in [[vaep]] — specifically the finding that scoring chances vary with goal difference.

Supported at the time by the EU Interreg VA project Nano4Sports.
