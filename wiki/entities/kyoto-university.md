# Kyoto University

Japanese research university. Its **Graduate School of Human and Environmental Studies** is the home department of [[kosuke-toda]] and [[keisuke-kushiro]], the two Kyoto-based authors of [[football-defence-evaluation-vdep|the VDEP paper]] (2022).

The departmental framing is worth noting. "Human and Environmental Studies" is a human-movement and behavioural-science setting rather than a computer-science one, and the collaboration structure reflects that — Kyoto supplying the sports-science grounding and supervision, [[nagoya-university]] supplying the machine-learning methodology through [[keisuke-fujii]].

That division shows in the paper's character: the target definitions ([[vdep|effective attack]] rather than shot; ball recovery as the positive event) are sports-domain judgements rather than modelling conveniences, and they are the part of VDEP most likely to outlast its specific classifiers.
