# INESC TEC (LIAAD)

Portuguese research institute based in Porto, associated with the [[universidade-do-porto|Universidade do Porto]]. The relevant unit for this vault is **LIAAD** — the Laboratory of Artificial Intelligence and Decision Support.

[[tiago-mendes-neves]] and [[joao-mendes-moreira]] hold joint FEUP/INESC TEC affiliations on [[football-performance-time-series|"Valuing Players Over Time"]], the standard Portuguese pattern of a university appointment paired with an institute one.

The paper records support from **FCT — Fundação para a Ciência e a Tecnologia**, the Portuguese national funding agency, under project UIDB/50014/2020 — the base funding line for the institute rather than a grant specific to football analytics. The football work sits inside a general machine-learning research programme rather than a dedicated sports-analytics one.
