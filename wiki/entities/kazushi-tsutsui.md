# Kazushi Tsutsui

Researcher at the Graduate School of Informatics, [[nagoya-university]]. Co-author of [[creating-scoring-opportunities-trajectory-prediction|the C-OBSO paper]] (Teranishi, Tsutsui, Takeda & Fujii).

## Position in the Fujii Group

Tsutsui appears on **both** of the group's counterfactual off-ball papers — the C-OBSO work held here, and the Umemoto, Tsutsui & Fujii GVDEP paper (arXiv:2212.00021) applying generalised defensive valuation to UEFA EURO.

That places him at the junction of the two lines: attacking space creation via trajectory prediction, and defensive valuation via location analysis. Whether that reflects a methodological specialism or coincidence of collaboration is not established by anything held here.

**Correction note.** Tsutsui was omitted from author lists on [[keisuke-fujii]] when those works were recorded from citation only. Recorded here on acquisition of the primary source.
