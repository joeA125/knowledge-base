# Floriane Magera

Researcher at EVS Broadcast Equipment and the University of Liège. Lead author of [[camera-calibration-benchmarking|ProCC]] (2025), which introduced the [[jac-metric|JaC metric]] and demonstrated that [[radial-distortion]] modelling is essential for professional sports [[camera-calibration]]. Co-author of [[soccernet-v2-action-spotting|Camera Calibration and Player Localization in SoccerNet-v2]] (Cioppa et al., 2021).
