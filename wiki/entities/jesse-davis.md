# Jesse Davis

Professor in the Department of Computer Science at KU Leuven, Belgium, and senior figure in the university's soccer analytics group. Research spans machine learning, data mining, and sports analytics.

Senior author on both vault sources from this group:
- [[evaluating-football-player-actions|Actions Speak Louder than Goals]] (KDD 2019) — introduced [[spadl]] and [[vaep]]
- [[on-ball-actions-football-xt-vs-vaep|Valuing On-the-Ball Actions in Soccer]] (AAAI 2020) — critical comparison of [[expected-threat|xT]] and VAEP

Also co-authored in-game win probability modelling for soccer (2019) and interpretable goal prediction (2019).

Partially supported by the EU Interreg VA project Nano4Sports, the KU Leuven Research Fund (C14/17/07), and the Research Foundation–Flanders (EOS No. 30992574).
