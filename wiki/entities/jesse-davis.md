# Jesse Davis

Professor at KU Leuven, Belgium. Senior author of [[evaluating-football-player-actions|Actions Speak Louder than Goals]] (KDD 2019), which introduced [[spadl]] and [[vaep]]. Research spans machine learning, data mining, and sports analytics. Partially supported by the EU Interreg VA Nano4Sports project.
