# Masakiyo Teranishi

Co-author of [[football-defence-evaluation-vdep|the VDEP paper]] (2022), Graduate School of Informatics, [[nagoya-university]]. Credited with data curation, software and manuscript review.

Also first author of Teranishi, [[keisuke-fujii|Fujii]] & Takeda, "Trajectory prediction with imitation learning reflecting defensive evaluation in team sports" (IEEE GCCE, 2020) — cited within the VDEP paper as prior work on rule-based defensive evaluation.

That earlier paper is worth noting as context: the Fujii group had already been working on defensive assessment via *trajectory* modelling before VDEP approached it through event prediction. The two lines share a concern — evaluating defenders who never touch the ball — and take different routes to it, one through movement, the other through outcome proxies.
