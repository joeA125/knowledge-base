# Robert Bajons

Researcher at the Institute for Statistics and Mathematics, Vienna University of Economics and Business (WU Wien). Joint first author of [[understanding-football-possessions-path-signatures|The Path to a Goal: Understanding Soccer Possessions via Path Signatures]] (2025), which introduced the [[sig-model]] and [[lpv]].

Also co-author (with Kurt Hornik) of work using possession sequences to estimate player strengths via regularised plus-minus regression (Bajons & Hornik, 2025) — a different route to player valuation from the same possession-as-unit premise that motivates the Sig-Model. Plus-minus methods attribute team outcomes to the players on the pitch rather than to individual actions, so they sidestep the [[action-valuation]] paradigm's attribution problem entirely at the cost of far coarser resolution. Not held in `raw/`.

Maintains the paper's implementation.
