# David Hirnschall

Researcher at the Institute for Statistics and Mathematics, Vienna University of Economics and Business (WU Wien). Corresponding author and joint first author of [[understanding-football-possessions-path-signatures|The Path to a Goal: Understanding Soccer Possessions via Path Signatures]] (2025), which introduced the [[sig-model]] and the [[lpv]] metric.

The work brings [[path-signature|path signatures]] — a tool from rough path theory previously applied in finance, medical diagnosis, and gesture recognition — to football event modelling for the first time.
