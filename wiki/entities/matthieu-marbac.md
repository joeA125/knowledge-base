# Matthieu Marbac

Researcher at Université Bretagne Sud, UMR CNRS 6205 (LMBA). Co-author of [[football-event-sequences-point-process-mixture|Model-Based Clustering of Football Event Sequences]] (2025).

A specialist in [[mixture-model|model-based clustering]], with prior work on double-partition clustering of multivariate functional data applied to swimming technique (Bouvet, El Kolei & Marbac, 2024) — the same methodological programme of adapting mixture models to the structure of sports data, applied to a different sport and data type.
